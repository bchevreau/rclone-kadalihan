rclone-kadalihan

A personal Google OAuth app used by its owner to connect rclone to Google Drive.

It supports owner-directed file transfers, backups and synchronization between Google Drive and storage selected by the owner. Access is limited by the permissions granted through Google.

This app is for the owner's personal use. It is not a public sign-up service or an official Google or rclone product.

Read the privacy policy for details about data access, storage and revoking access.
