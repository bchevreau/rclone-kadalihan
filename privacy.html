Privacy policy

rclone-kadalihan · Updated 1 October 2026

rclone-kadalihan is a personal Google OAuth app maintained by bchevreau for the owner's use with rclone and Google Drive.

Data access and use

Depending on the Google permissions granted and commands run, rclone may access Drive file contents and metadata, including names, folders, sizes and modification times. It may list, read, download, upload, modify or delete files to carry out the owner's requested transfers, backups and synchronization. Google handles sign-in; the app does not receive the Google account password.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are held in the rclone configuration or credential storage on systems where the owner runs rclone. File copies, caches and logs may remain on those systems or in storage destinations selected by the owner. Logs may contain filenames and operational metadata.

Data is used only for these owner-directed operations. It is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models. Transfers occur with Google and any storage providers the owner explicitly selects. This website does not receive Drive files or OAuth tokens.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls retention of copied files, caches, logs and configuration backups. These remain until the owner deletes them or the relevant storage provider's retention settings remove them. To stop access, remove this app in Google Account connections and delete its credentials from rclone and configuration backups. Revoking access does not automatically delete copies already transferred; delete those separately from each destination.

Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

This website

These informational pages contain no sign-in form, analytics scripts or advertising, and set no application cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process visitor technical data, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Contact and updates

Use the support email shown on the Google consent screen or the project's issue tracker for questions. Do not post private data or credentials in public issues. Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date.
